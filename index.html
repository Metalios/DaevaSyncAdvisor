    Data sources & limitations

    Each Sync Stone rerolls one selected Soul Binding line. Soul Codex: Reset (three books per full reset) and Manastones are separate systems. Sync Stones have no grades.

    For a constant, independent per-attempt probability p and n stones, the cumulative probability is 1 − (1 − p)n. It is not a guarantee; this model does not assume pity or adjust for changing odds. Recalculate if the per-attempt rate changes.

    The optional base estimates and suggested stat lists are carried over from v0.8, not verified single-line Sync data. Ordinary stat estimates use the inherited base weights, normally scaled by 0.8; named skill estimates assume 20% split across 12 active or 10 passive skills. Some item pools and jewelry weights are inferred. No probability is provided for unlisted items unless you enter one. Existing stat exclusions and Soul Fuse rates are not modeled. The extra Soul Fuse recommendation is a provisional next candidate from the ordinary pool; its exact eligibility is not independently verified. Equipment types are family presets, not a complete item database; choose only a matching family. Unsupported items have no inferred capacity.
