DAEVAARCHITECT / AION 2 GLOBAL

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy describes the current DaevaSyncAdvisor website and Discord Activity, maintained by the Metalios project. The advisor is an unofficial tool for AION 2 Global equipment recommendations and single-stat Sync Stone probability calculations.

Information processed by the calculator

You can use the calculator without creating an account. Equipment selections, class, build focus, selected stats, stone quantities and entered probabilities are processed in your browser. The current application does not transmit those inputs to a project-operated database or request your Discord identity, messages, contacts or OAuth authorization.

Local roll journal and exports

If you choose to record a roll, the journal stores the selected class, equipment slot and family, rarity, region, line type, Soul Fuse status, previous and resulting stats, a generated record identifier and timestamp in your browser's local storage, when available. These records persist until you remove them, clear your journal or clear site data. Older journal versions may remain in local storage; clearing all site data removes them too. Discord's embedded browser and your regular browser may keep separate copies.

CSV exports and copied summaries are generated locally at your request. They are not automatically sent to the developer or Discord. If you share them yourself, the recipient and platform you use may retain or redistribute them. Avoid including personal information in public reports.

Hosting and Discord

The application is hosted by Cloudflare. When you access it, hosting infrastructure may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses, browser information, request URLs and security or operational logs. When launched inside Discord, Discord and its embedded application proxy may process connection, session and Activity information under Discord's own policies. Our Activity uses Discord's Embedded App SDK to establish a connection and open external links when requested; it does not request OAuth access to your account.

See Cloudflare's privacy policy and Discord's privacy policy for their practices. External sites linked from the advisor have their own policies.

Sharing, retention and your choices

The current calculator has no project-operated central roll database, account system or advertising integration. We do not sell calculator inputs or local journal records. Local records remain under your control; the hosting and Discord platforms manage retention of their own operational data under their policies and applicable law. You can remove individual records, clear the journal or clear the app's site data in your browser settings.

Questions and changes

For questions about the application, privacy or deletion, contact the maintainer via the project's GitHub issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include personal information, account credentials or private records; request a private contact method if needed. We will update this policy before introducing materially different data collection, including any shared roll database.
